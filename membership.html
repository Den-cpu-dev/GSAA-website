Become a Member of GSAA

Membership is open to sociologists, anthropologists, social workers, and all persons interested in advancing the aims of the Association.

Why Join

The Value of Membership

Journal Access
Full digital access to the GSAA Journal archive and advance access to new issues before publication.
Professional Network
Connect with 500+ sociologists and anthropologists across all 16 regions of Ghana and an international affiliate network.
Event Discounts
Reduced registration fees for all GSAA conferences, workshops, and public lectures, plus priority access to limited-capacity events.
Research Grants
Eligibility to apply for GSAA small research grants, the Early Career Award, and travel grants for international conferences.
Categories

Membership Categories & Fees

Annual dues are based on GSAA's current membership categories and are payable by 31st March each year.

Graduate Students
Graduate Student Member
GHS 100 / year
For graduate students in sociology, anthropology, social work, and related social science fields.
  • Journal access (digital)
  • Conference registration discount (50%)
  • GSAA student network
  • Mentorship programme eligibility
Core Category
Academics
Academic Member
GHS 200 / year
For teaching and practicing academics in sociology, anthropology, social work, and related social sciences.
  • Journal access (digital + print)
  • Conference discount (30%)
  • Voting rights & governance
  • Research grant eligibility
  • GSAA directory listing
  • Peer collaboration network
Non-Academics
Non-Academic Member
GHS 300 / year
For professionals and practitioners outside formal academic appointments who support GSAA's objectives.
  • Journal access (digital)
  • Conference participation eligibility
  • Professional network access
  • Policy dialogue participation
Honorary
Honorary Member
GHS 400 / year
For designated honorary membership category under GSAA's current dues framework.
  • Journal access (digital)
  • Conference participation eligibility
  • Association recognition category
  • Network engagement
External Scholars
External Scholar Member
USD 50 / year
For scholars outside Ghana interested in Ghanaian and African sociology, anthropology, and social work scholarship.
  • Journal access (digital)
  • Conference participation eligibility
  • International network linkage
  • Association updates and notices
Membership Note
Anchor Universities
Mandated membership
Teaching staff in UG, KNUST, UCC, and UDS are mandated to be members of the Association.
  • Institution-based representation
  • Management Committee participation
  • Support for annual rotational hosting
