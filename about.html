1963 – 2012

Foundational Origins & Disciplinary Expansion

The intellectual heritage of the association traces back to 1963 with the establishment of the Ghana Sociological Association (GSA). Founded during an era of profound socio-political transformation following Ghana’s independence, the GSA served as the premier national body dedicated to fostering sociological teaching, rigorous field research, and social policy dialogue across the country’s universities.

For nearly five decades, the association provided an intellectual anchor for generations of Ghanaian sociologists who examined chieftaincy, agrarian transformation, industrialization, migration, kinship, and family dynamics. Recognizing the evolving epistemological landscape and the deep synergy between sociology, anthropology, and applied social work, a historic decision was taken on 15th June 2012: the association formally reconstituted and expanded its constitutional mandate to become the Ghana Sociological and Anthropological Association (GSAA). This expansion united sociologists, anthropologists, and social development practitioners into a unified, multidisciplinary national platform.

2013 – 2019

The Re-Launch Symposium & Institutional Momentum

On 1 March 2013, the GSAA marked its official resurgence with a landmark Re-Launch Symposium held at Yiri Lodge, University of Ghana, Legon, under the theme “Sociology and National Development.” The symposium re-energized academic departments nationwide, re-established institutional governance structures, and inaugurated a rotating national conference model across Ghana’s leading public universities.

In September 2020, amidst widespread national and international outcry over tragic incidents of gendered violence and witchcamp abuses in Ghana—most notably the incident at Kafaba—the GSAA demonstrated the vital public role of social science scholarship by convening a high-impact national virtual seminar.

2021 – 2023

Pandemic Resilience & The 7th Annual Conference at UCC

As global social life confronted the unprecedented disruptions of the COVID-19 pandemic, the GSAA sustained continuous academic dialogue. In September 2021, the 5th Annual Conference was convened virtually under the theme “Ghanaian Social Life and the COVID-19 Pandemic,” followed in September 2022 by the 6th Annual Conference at the University of Ghana, Legon, addressing “Sociology and Anthropology in the Era of New Global Emergencies.”

In September 2023, the association convened in person for its landmark 7th Annual Conference at the University of Cape Coast, critically confronting the accelerating digital age.

2025 & Beyond

Contemporary Disciplinary Leadership & Global Outlook

In August 2025, the Department of Sociology at the University of Ghana, Legon, hosted the 9th Annual Conference, demonstrating the enduring intellectual vitality and cross-generational collaboration within the association.

Today, under the leadership of its National Convenor and institutional Management Committee, the GSAA continues to expand its global footprint through institutional partnerships with the International Sociological Association (ISA)—including joint publishing workshops and international research networks—while curating the Ghana Journal of Sociology and Anthropology on open-access platforms such as ScienceOpen. Over six decades since its founding, the association remains steadfast in its mission: elevating social science scholarship into transformative public action for Ghana and Africa.
