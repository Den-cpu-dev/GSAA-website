Our Story

GSAA Overview

The Ghana Sociological Association (GSA) was started in 1963. On 15th June 2012, the Ghana Sociological and Anthropological Association (GSAA) was formed, expanding the platform to integrate sociology, anthropology, and social work across Ghana.

Vision & Mission

Empowering Minds,
Shaping Society

Vision Statement "To be the leading platform for advancing sociological, anthropological and social work knowledge and practice in Ghana and beyond."
Mission Statement "To bring together scholars and practitioners of sociology, anthropology and social work to promote critical engagement with social issues, and foster impactful research, teaching, and collaboration."
Aims

Association Priorities

Mobilize & Connect
Bring together sociologists, anthropologists, social workers, and related social scientists in Ghana.
Exchange & Networking
Serve as a forum for critical dialogue, idea exchange, and professional networking among members.
Teaching & Learning
Promote sociology, anthropology, and social work teaching in public and private higher education institutions.
Journal Development
Strengthen the Ghana Journal of Sociology and Anthropology as a mouthpiece for quality peer-reviewed scholarship. Browse Collection on ScienceOpen ↗
History

Re-Launch and Growth

1963
Establishment of GSA
The Ghana Sociological Association (GSA) was started in 1963 as the premier national body for sociological research and teaching in Ghana.
2012
Formation of GSAA
On 15th June 2012, the Ghana Sociological and Anthropological Association (GSAA) was formed, expanding the body beyond sociology to include anthropologists and social workers.
2013
Re-Launch Symposium
On 1 March 2013, the association held its re-launch at Yiri Lodge, University of Ghana, under the theme "Sociology and National Development."
2015
1st Annual Conference
Hosted in November 2015 at the University of Ghana, Legon, focusing on "Social Science Knowledge Production in Africa: Interrogating the Past for Future Directions and Actions."
2017
2nd Annual Conference
Hosted in September 2017 at the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Kumasi, under the theme "Discourses and Debates in Contemporary Africa: The Social Scientist and Sustainable Development."
2018
3rd Annual Conference
Hosted in September 2018 at the University of Cape Coast (UCC), Cape Coast, under the theme "The Social Sciences and the Sustainable Development Goals."
2019
4th Annual Conference
Hosted in September 2019 at the University of Ghana, Legon, on the theme "Re-positioning Sociology and Anthropology for National Development."
2020
Annual National Seminar
Held virtually in September 2020, focusing on "Witchcraft beliefs, Accusation and Associated violence in Ghana: Sociological and Anthropological Perspectives."
2021
5th Annual Conference
Held as a virtual conference in September 2021, exploring the theme "Ghanaian Social Life and the COVID-19 Pandemic."
2022
6th Annual Conference
Hosted in September 2022 at the University of Ghana, Legon, under the theme "Sociology and Anthropology in the Era of New Global Emergencies."
2023
7th Annual Conference
Hosted in September 2023 at the University of Cape Coast (UCC), Cape Coast, on the theme "Digital Technologies in Sociological and Anthropological Spaces."
2025
9th Annual Conference
Hosted by the Sociology Department, University of Ghana, Legon, in August 2025, continuing broad disciplinary engagement across sociological and anthropological spaces.
Current
Conference & Policy Engagement
Conference papers and GSAA deliberations continue to address social issues of national importance and strengthen evidence-based social science engagement in Ghana.
Leadership

Association Structure

The GSAA is led by a National Convenor and governed through institutional representation from member universities. The Management Committee includes departmental heads and representatives from anchor institutions who work together to run GSAA affairs.

Constitution &
Governance

The affairs of GSAA are currently guided by an Interim Constitution, which remains in force until a permanent constitution is ratified at an annual meeting of the Association.

This framework guides governance, administration, and membership accountability while the association continues institutional consolidation and growth.
