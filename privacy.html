Privacy Policy

How GSAA collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. Who We Are

The Ghana Sociological and Anthropological Association ("GSAA", "we", "us", "our") is a professional association registered in Ghana. Our registered address is: Department of Sociology, University of Ghana, Legon, P.O. Box LG 65, Accra, Ghana.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you:

  • Apply for or renew membership
  • Register for events, conferences, or workshops
  • Submit articles or papers to the GSAA Journal
  • Contact us via our website or by email
  • Subscribe to our newsletter

The types of data we collect include: name, email address, telephone number, institutional affiliation, academic qualifications, research interests, payment information (processed securely by third-party processors), and correspondence with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage your membership
  • To send event information, newsletters, and academic announcements (with your consent)
  • To process journal submissions and peer review
  • To comply with legal and financial obligations
  • To improve our website and services

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (membership and event registration), your consent (newsletter subscriptions), and our legitimate interests in operating as a professional association.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with: service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., email platforms, event registration systems), and co-organising institutions for specific events. All third-party processors are contractually required to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

Member data is retained for the duration of membership and up to 5 years thereafter for legal and archival purposes. Event registration data is retained for 3 years. Contact form data is retained for 2 years.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, withdraw consent at any time, and lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@gsaa.org.gh.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified to members by email. Continued use of our services following notification constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries: privacy@gsaa.org.gh or write to our postal address above.
