Our Purpose

Empowering Minds,
Shaping Society

GSAA provides a national platform for mobilizing and connecting sociologists, anthropologists, social work scholars, and practitioners across Ghana to promote critical dialogue, scholarship, and public impact.

Vision Statement
To be the leading platform for advancing sociological, anthropological and social work knowledge and practice in Ghana and beyond.
Mission Statement
To bring together scholars and practitioners of sociology, anthropology and social work to promote critical engagement with social issues, and foster impactful research, teaching, and collaboration.
